The honesty rail. This course separates three kinds of claims and labels them: what research supports (with the actual effect sizes), what tradition says (marked "traditionally said to"), and what is marketing. Meditation is a skill worth learning - it is not a cure-all, it is not for everyone, and we will say so plainly.
