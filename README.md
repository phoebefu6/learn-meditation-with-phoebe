# learn meditation with phoebe

A secular, honest on-ramp to a real meditation practice - six 45-minute sessions, by Phoebe Fu.

**Live site:** https://phoebefu6.github.io/learn-meditation-with-phoebe/

## What this is

Meditation for skeptics: the breath, the body, the wandering mind, and the styles beyond -
with plain talk about what research really shows (actual effect sizes, failed replications,
adverse-effect rates), what tradition claims (marked "traditionally said to"), and what is
marketing. Learners leave with their own 4-week starter practice plan, built one step per
session.

## The sessions

1. **Why meditate** - what it is, isn't, and what the research shows
2. **The breath** - your anchor + the built-in breath pacer (a real tool, nothing simulated)
3. **Body & posture** - the body scan and how to sit (chairs count)
4. **The wandering mind** - the actual skill you are training
5. **Beyond the breath** - styles, traditions, and picking yours
6. **Making it stick** - habit, apps, cautions & your 4-week plan

## Format

Static HTML/CSS/JS, no build step. Each session: 45-min live arc (welcome / concepts /
practice / Q&A), inline SVG diagrams, a 3-question quiz, and a pin-able cheat sheet.
Session 2 hosts `med-live.js`, an offline breath pacer with four patterns (4-6 relaxing,
box, coherent ~5.5, 4-7-8) - real timing, real session clock, honest evidence grades.

Part of [Learn with Phoebe](https://phoebefu6.github.io/learn-with-phoebe/).
